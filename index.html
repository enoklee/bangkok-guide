🛡️ 3 Rules to Survive Bangkok Like an Expat

  • 1. Never hail roadside cabs during rush hour (17:00–20:00). Take the BTS Skytrain or MRT subway. If you must use cars, download Grab or Bolt to avoid fixed-fare scams.
  • 2. Cash is still king for street stalls. Carry 20, 50, and 100 THB banknotes. Most vendors don't accept foreign credit cards.
  • 3. Book popular tours 48+ hours ahead. Peak season (Nov–Feb) means the best small-group vans and dinner cruises sell out days in advance.

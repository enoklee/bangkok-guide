🛡️ 3 Rules to Survive Bangkok Like an Expat

  • 1. Never hail roadside cabs during rush hour (17:00–20:00). Take the BTS Skytrain or MRT subway. For midnight rides after socials, use Grab or Bolt.
  • 2. Bring hard-sole shoes or dance sneakers. Wood and tile floors in Bangkok clubs can be slick. Suede soles get damaged quickly in tropical rainstorms.
  • 3. Book popular tours 48+ hours ahead. Peak season means the best small-group vans and dinner cruises sell out days in advance.
